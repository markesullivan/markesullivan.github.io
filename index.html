quokka

quokka is a personal job-tracking script. It is used by one person, its developer, on their own computer. It is not a public service and has no other users.

What it does

The script reads one personal Gmail mailbox, which holds job-alert emails and application status emails forwarded by its owner. It records the job listings and status changes it finds in the owner's own Google spreadsheet. It also adds or removes two labels on those emails so it does not process them twice.

Privacy policy

  • Access. quokka asks Google for permission to read the owner's mailbox and change labels on its messages. It does not send email and does not delete email.
  • Storage. Email content is processed and stored only on the owner's computer and in the owner's own spreadsheet. The sign-in token is kept on the owner's computer only.
  • Sharing. quokka does not share, sell, or transfer any Google user data to anyone else. It does not use that data for advertising.
  • Revoking access. The owner can remove quokka's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
